Before the brand, there was the factory. Vit Makanan (K.L.) Sdn. Bhd. has made noodles in Kuala Lumpur since 1975, halal certified since 1980, and quietly feeds a country that knows the yellow pack by sight.

  1. 1975

    The family starts making noodles in Kuala Lumpur. The phoenix goes on the first wrapper.

  2. 1980

    Halal certified by JAKIM. 46 years unbroken, with preferential access across the Gulf.

  3. 70%

    Of revenue is the Malaysian plain noodle base. The home market is served first, always.

  4. 30 years

    Of export experience across dozens of markets.
