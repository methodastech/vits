FAQ

The questions the trade desk hears

What is the minimum order quantity for private label noodle manufacturing?

The MOQ typically starts from 1 full container load (FCL), depending on packaging type and customization. Smaller trial runs are available.

How long does it take to produce private label instant noodles from start to finish?

The complete production cycle from product development to shipment takes approximately 12 to 16 weeks. Repeat orders may complete faster.

Is there a difference between private label and OEM noodle manufacturing?

Yes. Private label uses our existing formulas and production with your branding. OEM involves full customization of the recipe, flavor, and packaging.

Can I customize the instant noodle recipe for my private label brand?

Yes, we offer full customization of noodle texture, flavor profile, and seasoning. The R&D team assists with spicy, vegetarian, healthy, or regional taste preferences.

What packaging types are available for private label noodles?

We provide a range of packaging options to match your brand's needs, including stand-up pouches, plastic film, and kraft paper noodle boxes.

Are Vit's OEM noodles certified with halal, HACCP, or other food safety standards?

Yes. Our factory is certified HALAL (JAKIM), HACCP, GMP, and FSSC 22000.

Do you export private label noodles internationally?

Yes, Vit's exports worldwide, in compliance with HALAL, HACCP, GMP, FSSC 22000 and FDA requirements, and is SEDEX audited.

Can I request product samples or visit the factory before ordering?

Yes. We offer free samples for taste and quality testing. Factory visits can also be arranged upon request.

Can Vit's manufacture noodles for foodservice, restaurant chains, or bulk supply?

Absolutely. We supply OEM noodles in bulk for restaurants, foodservice companies, and industrial kitchens.

Do you assist with food labeling requirements and compliance?

Yes. We provide advice on food labeling requirements, including ingredient lists, nutritional facts, allergen declarations, and barcode integration.

What kind of support will I receive as a private label client?

You'll have a dedicated account manager to assist with R&D, sampling, pricing, packaging, and export logistics.

How do I start a private label partnership with Vit's?

Contact the trade desk with details about your brand, product requirements, and estimated volume. The form on the home page goes straight to them.
