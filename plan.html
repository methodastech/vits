02
The strategy the site carries
Three jobs, in payment order
Job 1 · prove capability

Answer "can you make Buldak?" before the email

Buyers open with that question. The site's answer: a seasoning capability story with the curry range as exhibit A, the heat ladder as the demonstration, and the factory's certifications underneath. Curry is the vehicle, capability is the message.

Job 2 · publish the numbers

MOQ tiers, capacity, lead time

The five things a buyer checks, published plainly: formats, shelf life, certifications, MOQ, customisation. Nobody branded does this in Malaysia. Figures owed by operations, gated in red in the content plan.

Job 3 · protect the base

Malaysia sees improvement, not repositioning

The Malaysian site keeps its consumer face, gains speed, schema, weights and the recipe hub, and loses nothing: every URL is mapped and redirected. The plain-noodle business that pays for everything is untouched.

How this becomes the best trade site in the category

The ambition, concretely: the only branded Malaysian noodle manufacturer whose site answers all five buyer checks on one page, in under three seconds, with a product experience no rival attempts. The capability-gap table is why the bar is reachable.

05
The landing page, scene by scene
Bazil's narrative, the four decoded references, one specified journey

The order is the client's own: product, then company, then the trade offer, then the experience. The motion grammar is taken from four studied references: the Lagunitas IPA page (pinned product, sections scroll past it, ends in a noodle bowl), the Tubik drinks hero (display type behind product, range fly-in), the Flora specimen gallery (one centred product, crossfade), and the scroll-rotating 3D can demo. Not any particular parallax: a journey with a beginning, a middle and a bowl.

1

The pack, floating in the light

3D pack · slow idle spin · display type behind product

Cream paper background. Giant condensed display type, THE YELLOW PACK OF MALAYSIA, runs edge to edge BEHIND the real-time 3D economy pack, which floats tilted, slowly turning, catching light on the film. One line below: 51 years, one factory, halal since 1980. Stat chips right: 700g, 10 cakes, since 1975 pending verification. The scroll cue is a noodle strand drawn as a line.

Reference: Tubik hero, type-behind-product. Lagunitas stat card.

2

What the product is

Scroll-scrub rotation · annotation callouts fan out

The pack straightens and scales up as the headline slides away. Splat-shaped callouts fan out around it, wheat flour and palm oil, the noodle window, the phoenix mark, 10 cakes one decision, halal mark. The pack answers the shelf question: this is the noodle Malaysia already knows.

Reference: Lagunitas ingredient splats. Claude-built can, scroll-linked rotation.

3

Who makes it, 51 years of it

Pinned pack shrinks to a corner · timeline band scrolls

The pack docks small in the corner like a wax seal while the company story passes: 1975 the family starts, 1980 halal certified JAKIM, the plant, 30 years exporting, four exhibitions a year. Dated fact tiles, warm archival tone, one sentence each. No claim without a source.

Reference: Lagunitas brewer-video section, product pinned while story scrolls.

4

The trade offer, said plainly

Colour flips to dark · carton stack builds on scroll

The page turns kraft-and-dark: the OEM carton, modelled from the real Economy Pack carton, stacks into a pallet wall as you scroll. Over it, the offer: OEM, private label, bulk. The five buyer checks as a row of cards: formats, shelf life, certifications, MOQ tiers, customisation. MOQ figures red-gated until operations confirms. CTA: talk to the trade desk.

Reference: the factory band in the 3D can demo. The carton is our own asset, photographed from their site.

5

The range, one specimen at a time

Dark specimen stage · crossfade between SKUs

Museum-dark band. One pack centred at a time, flavour name in display serif, origin and heat meta below, texture close-ups right. Crossfade through the lines: Economy, Premium Penang range, Air-dried Healthy Series, Hotlicious Pan Mee, Italian series. The air-dried card carries its spec: air-dried, trans-fat-free, coloured by real spinach and tomato.

Reference: Flora specimen gallery, dark, serif, crossfade.

6

The cook

The showpiece · scroll-scrubbed 3D sequence

Back to light. The economy pack returns centre stage, larger. As the visitor scrolls: the pack tilts, the wrapper opens, a noodle cake slides out and drops into a waiting bowl, steam rises, strands loosen and swirl. The scrub runs both directions, scroll up and it un-cooks. Copy is minimal: three time markers, 3 minutes, one pot, one meal. This single scene is the pitch's proof that rich motion can be code-light.

Reference: Bazil's brief verbatim. Lagunitas finale bowl. Every beat scroll-scrubbed, reversible, 60fps.

7

The bowl, and the ask

Settle · steam idles · enquiry form

The finished bowl sits centred, steam idling, chopsticks lifting a strand. Beside it the closing line, one factory, any flavour your market wants, and the trade enquiry form with the exhibition calendar: Gulfood 15 to 19 March 2027, then the US, Bangkok, UK. Footer carries the one canonical brand name, the certifications, and nothing else.

Reference: Lagunitas "Beer speaks" recipe close. Flora's calm.

Copy voice for the whole page: confident, not shouting. Short declaratives. Malay phrases where they earn their place. No exclamation marks, no unverified claims, heat described by rung not by dare.
06
Motion and 3D, engineered
How the showpiece stays light, editable and reversible
Stack

Code, not video

GSAP ScrollTrigger for the journey, Three.js for the pack, carton and bowl. No video payloads anywhere: the entire motion system is procedural, which is why the page can carry a full 3D journey in less weight than the current homepage's image payload.

Budget

The numbers the demo must hit

First paint under 2s on mid-range mobile, scroll at 60fps with DPR capped, JS under 350KB gzipped including Three.js, textures under 600KB total, one canvas, draw calls under 60. Static poster fallback for reduced-motion and old devices.

Reversibility

Every beat scrubbed, nothing fired

All journey animation is scroll-scrubbed, not time-triggered: scroll up and the noodle un-cooks. This kills the two classic failures, animations that start too late and animations that break when the visitor changes direction.

Editability

The CMS contract

Every headline, paragraph, stat and image on the page is a content field. The 3D scene reads its copy from the same fields. The client edits text and swaps images without touching the motion system, which answers the "hard to update" objection in writing.

07
SEO preservation and the migration
The answer to the fear the client raised twice

Two moves, in order of risk. Move one carries no risk at all: the trade destination is a new subdomain, nothing on vitsnoodles.com changes, every existing ranking stays where it is. Move two is the rebuild, and it is a mapped migration, not a redesign: all 127 URLs inventoried in the web audit carry a 301 redirect to their successor, content transfers intact, and the build adds what Yoast never had.

StepWhat happensProof it worked
1 · Freeze & crawlFull crawl of all 127 URLs, titles, metas, schema and inlinks archived before anything movesThe archive itself, delivered
2 · MapEvery URL assigned a successor: kept, merged, or improved. Nothing dropped silentlyThe 301 map, 127 rows, client-visible
3 · Carry the fundamentalsTitles, metas, H1s, canonicals migrate with the contentDiff report, old vs new
4 · Add what was missingOrganization, Product and Recipe schema, hreflang, og:image, product metas, alt textRich results test passes per template
5 · Launch & verifyRedirects live, sitemap resubmitted, Search Console monitoredZero 404s in the crawl, week one and week four checks
Scope rule, stated in the proposal and repeated here: SEO optimisation is included in the build, schema, hreflang, metadata, alt text, speed and redirects. Ongoing SEO is a separate package, quoted after the site ships or when the client asks for it.

09
Look and feel
The design language, drawn from the pack, not imposed on it
Colour

The pack is the palette

Cream paper base, the pack's yellow as the field colour, Vit's red for type and accents, kraft brown for the trade band, museum-dark for the specimen stage. Nothing outside the pack's own world.

Type

Big, confident, bilingual

A condensed display face for the giant headlines, a working sans for body, monospace for spec labels. EN leads, BM phrases where they earn their place, consistent with the pack's own bilingual habit.

Imagery

Real product, drawn accents

The 3D pack and carton are modelled on the real artwork. Illustrated accents, steam wisps, noodle-strand lines, the phoenix, come from the pack. No stock photography, no AI-look imagery: the client named that fear and the answer is craft.

What happens after launch
The site as an operating asset, not a delivered file
Measurement

Buyer signals, not vanity

Search Console and analytics wired at launch. The KPIs are trade KPIs: enquiries sent, MOQ page depth, follow-up meetings booked per show, plus zero 404s at migration and rich-result impressions on recipes.

Care

Kept current in buyer time

Exhibition calendar and trade documents updated per show cycle. Care plan from the standing rate card, reactive support free, proactive care paid, quoted separately as always.

Growth, when earned

E-commerce and channels

WooCommerce is already installed: the D2C shop and TikTok Shop channel activate on lane 2 when the client calls for it, on the plumbing this build leaves clean.

Ongoing SEO

A separate conversation

Optimisation ships in the build. An ongoing SEO retainer, content, links, query mining, is quoted only after the site is live or when the client asks, per the standing rule.
