About · Vit Makanan (K.L.) Sdn. Bhd.

From a kitchen in Selayang to 30 countries

Vit's was founded in 1975 by Mr. Lai Ah Lek and Mdm. Lee Kim Eng in a small family kitchen in Selayang, and grew into Vit Makanan. The name comes from the Mandarin Wei Yi, the only one. The phoenix on every pack stands for power, renewal and longevity.

Milestones

Fifty years, eight turns

1975

Humble beginnings

Founded in a small family kitchen in Selayang by Mr. Lai Ah Lek and Mdm. Lee Kim Eng. The business later becomes Vit Makanan.

1980

Certified halal

Halal certification, to strengthen consumer trust and serve every community.

1998

Expanding horizons

Relocation to a 105,000 sq ft facility in Rawang to meet rising international demand.

2006

Raising standards

HACCP and GMP certifications, awarded by Moody International.

2015

Fresh noodles

Fresh-cooked ramen and udon join the range, beyond instant noodles.

2020

Italian series

A fried instant noodle line in Aglio Olio, Bolognese and Carbonara.

2021

Through the pandemic

Operations continue, and 1,500 households in Rawang are supported.

2025

MIFT Gold Award

Gold Award for Product Innovation.
