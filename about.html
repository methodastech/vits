About us

The noodle
Malaysia grew up on

Vit Makanan (K.L.) Sdn. Bhd. has made noodles in Kuala Lumpur since 1975. One factory, one family recipe, and a yellow pack a whole country recognises by sight. Today that same line runs export orders under our label and yours.

01 · The factory

A seasoning kitchen, not just a noodle line

Most factories make noodles. Fewer can develop the taste that goes on them. Our kitchen speaks curry, tom yam, rendang, white pepper and beyond, which is why a buyer can walk in with a flavour brief and walk out with a sample.

Fried and air-dried lines run side by side, so the same building serves a value pillow pack and a trans-fat-free kraft carton without compromise on either.
